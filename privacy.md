# Focus — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 10 November 2026*

Focus is a menu bar timer for macOS made by Kenneth Egholm. It is built to work entirely on your Mac.

## What Focus collects

Nothing. Focus has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no servers. It never sends any data anywhere.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Settings** (which switches are on, the calendar you picked, the text in "What are you working on?") are stored locally on your Mac.
- **Calendar** — only if you turn on "Use calendar" or "Block time in calendar" and allow access:
  - Focus reads today's events on your Mac to find your next meeting. It doesn't store or send them.
  - With "Block time in calendar" on, Focus adds one busy event per session, titled with what you're working on (or "Focusing – DND"), to the calendar you picked — and shortens or removes it afterwards. That calendar's own sync (for example iCloud, Google or Exchange) shares it the way it shares any event you create; that's the point of the feature.
- **Do Not Disturb** — if you turn on "Focus during sessions", Focus runs two Shortcuts named "Focus On" and "Focus Off" on your Mac.
- **Notifications** — the "Time's up" notification is created on your Mac.
